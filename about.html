Our Story

A Table Set With Intention

Inside was born from a simple idea: that the best evenings happen when good food, unhurried company, and a warm room come together. Just steps from Batumi's seafront, we've built a place that feels like stepping out of the noise — and into something more honest.

How It Started

Inside opened its doors in 2021, founded by a small team of friends who grew up on Adjarian home cooking and wanted to bring that same generosity to a proper table. What began as a handful of recipes passed down from grandmothers has grown into a full kitchen — one that still insists on doing things the slow way: stocks simmered overnight, bread baked each morning, and produce sourced from growers we actually know by name.

The name "Inside" is a small nod to what we're really offering — not just a meal, but a sense of being let in. Inside the kitchen, inside the tradition, inside a moment that isn't rushed.

"We didn't want to reinvent Adjarian cooking — we wanted to give it the room it deserves."

— Founding Chef, Inside
